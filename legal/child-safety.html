BizMingle Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 15, 2026
Effective date: October 15, 2026
Developer: John Solace

BizMingle is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We explicitly prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), grooming, child trafficking for sexual exploitation, sextortion, and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We have zero tolerance for this conduct or content.

Reporting and feedback

Users can report and block people from the options menu in dating chat. For urgent safety concerns or reports involving a child, contact our designated Child Safety Point of Contact at ayodeleogunyemi25@yahoo.co.uk and include enough information for us to locate the account or content. Do not send suspected CSAM as an attachment.

Review and enforcement

We review safety reports, remove prohibited content where appropriate, restrict or terminate offending accounts, and preserve relevant records when required for an investigation or by law. If we obtain actual knowledge of CSAM, we take action in accordance with these standards and applicable law, including reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children or the relevant regional authority where required.
